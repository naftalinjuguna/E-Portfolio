— Teaching Philosophy

Three beliefs that

guide my practice

01
Every Learner Belongs

Inclusion isn't a policy — it's a mindset. I design every lesson with full spectrum of learning needs in mind, ensuring no child feels invisible, rushed, or left behind. Differentiated instruction is my baseline, not my exception.

02
Curiosity Before Content

Before I teach a concept, I try to spark a question. When students ask "why?" on their own, they're already learning. I use inquiry-based approaches, real-world problems, and open-ended discussion to keep curiosity alive

03
Community Shapes Learning

The Classroom is a microcosm of society. I actively build classroom communities where empathy, collaboration, and respect are practiced daily - because character development and academic achivement go hand in hand.
